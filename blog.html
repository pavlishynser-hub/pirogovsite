Prague Airport Transfer: Complete Guide 2026

January 25, 2026 • 5 min read

Prague Václav Havel Airport (PRG) is the main international airport serving the Czech Republic, located approximately 17 km from the city center. Whether you're arriving for business or pleasure, getting to your destination smoothly is essential for starting your trip right.

Transfer Options Compared

Private Transfer (Recommended): The most comfortable option. A driver meets you at arrivals with a name sign, helps with luggage, and takes you directly to your hotel. Price: from €35 for up to 4 passengers. Travel time: 25-35 minutes.

Public Transport: Bus 119 connects the airport to Nádraží Veleslavín metro station, then take metro line A to the center. Price: €1.50. Travel time: 45-60 minutes. Not recommended with heavy luggage.

Taxi: Available at the airport, but prices vary significantly. Always agree on a price before getting in. Typical price to center: €25-40.

Pro Tips

  • Book your transfer in advance, especially during peak season (May-September)
  • Terminal 1 serves non-Schengen flights, Terminal 2 serves Schengen flights
  • Rush hour (7-9 AM, 4-7 PM) can add 15-20 minutes to your journey

Prague to Vienna: The Perfect Day Trip

January 20, 2026 • 7 min read

Vienna, the imperial capital of Austria, is just 4 hours from Prague by car — making it perfect for a day trip. With proper planning, you can experience the highlights of this magnificent city and return the same day.

Suggested Itinerary

6:00 AM — Depart from Prague. Enjoy the scenic drive through Moravia.
10:00 AM — Arrive in Vienna. Start at Schönbrunn Palace, the former imperial summer residence.
12:30 PM — Lunch in the city center. Try Wiener Schnitzel at a traditional restaurant.
2:00 PM — Walk along the Ringstrasse, see the Opera House, and visit St. Stephen's Cathedral.
4:30 PM — Coffee and Sachertorte at a classic Viennese café.
6:00 PM — Depart Vienna.
10:00 PM — Arrive back in Prague.

Why Choose Private Transfer?

  • Flexibility to stop at scenic viewpoints along the way
  • Door-to-door service — no need to navigate train stations
  • Comfortable rest during the 4-hour journey
  • Local driver tips and recommendations

10 Tips for Stress-Free European Travel

January 5, 2026 • 6 min read

After 10+ years of driving passengers across Europe, our team has collected invaluable insights. Here are our top tips for making your European journey smooth and enjoyable.

1. Book Transfers in Advance

Especially during peak season (May-September) and holidays. Last-minute bookings may cost more or be unavailable.

2. Pack Light, Travel Easy

European cities often have cobblestone streets and stairs. One carry-on and one personal item is ideal for most trips.

3. Keep Documents Accessible

Passport, booking confirmations, and travel insurance should be easily accessible. Digital copies in your phone are helpful backups.

4. Inform Your Driver About Stops

Need a bathroom break or photo stop? Just ask! Our drivers are happy to accommodate reasonable requests along the route.

5. Download Offline Maps

Google Maps or Maps.me work offline. Download maps of your destination before you leave — roaming charges can be expensive.

6. Carry Some Cash

While cards are widely accepted, some smaller shops, markets, and tips work better with local currency.

7. Account for Time Zones

Central European Time (CET) applies to most countries we serve. Remember to adjust if coming from a different zone.

8. Stay Hydrated

Long journeys can be dehydrating. Bring a water bottle — our vehicles have space for your drinks and snacks.

9. Be On Time

For airport transfers, we recommend being ready 10 minutes before the scheduled pickup. It helps everyone stay on schedule.

10. Enjoy the Journey

The landscapes between cities are beautiful! Put away your phone sometimes and enjoy the view — you're traveling through Europe!
